Shipping info

Free shipping on every order. Honest timelines, tracking included.

Delivery times

Orders ship from our supply partners within 1–2 business days and arrive in 7–12 business days. We'd love to promise 2-day delivery, but we'd rather give you a timeline we can actually hit.

Shipping cost

Free. Every order, every product, no minimum.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse (usually within 1–2 business days of ordering). If you haven't received tracking after 2 business days, email us at roman.proposal@gmail.com.

Where we ship

We currently ship within the United States.

Delays

Occasionally a package is delayed in transit. If your order hasn't arrived within 15 business days, contact us and we'll investigate — and if it's lost, we'll reship or refund you, your choice.
