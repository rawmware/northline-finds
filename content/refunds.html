Returns & refunds

30 days. No questions asked. No restocking fees.

The short version

If you're not happy, return any unused item in its original condition within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. That's it.

How to start a return

  1. Email roman.proposal@gmail.com with your order details and the item(s) you'd like to return.
  2. We'll reply within 1 business day with return instructions.
  3. Once we receive the item (or proof of return shipment), we refund your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Damaged or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, email us a photo and we'll make it right immediately — replacement or full refund, your choice, and we cover the return shipping.

Return shipping

For change-of-mind returns, the customer covers return postage. For our mistakes (damaged, defective, or wrong items), we cover it.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened personal-care items can't be returned unless they're defective. Everything else in the store is returnable.
