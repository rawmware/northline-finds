Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

How long does shipping take?
Orders ship from our supply partners within 1–2 business days and arrive in 7–12 business days. Shipping is free on every order, and you'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves the warehouse.
Where do products ship from?
Our products are fulfilled directly by vetted supply partners, which lets us keep prices low. That's why delivery takes 7–12 business days instead of 2 — we'd rather be honest about the timeline than surprise you.
How do I pay?
Through Stripe's secure checkout — the same payment processor used by millions of businesses. We accept all major credit and debit cards. We never see or store your card details.
What is your return policy?
30 days, no questions asked, for any unused item in its original condition. If your item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, we cover return shipping and refund you in full. See our returns & refunds page for the full policy.
How do I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking number by email within 1–2 business days of ordering. If it hasn't arrived after 2 business days, email us at roman.proposal@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
Is this site legit?
Yes. We're a real small business. Every product is from a supplier we've vetted, every payment goes through Stripe's secure checkout, and a real person answers every email. If anything goes wrong with your order, we make it right — that's the whole business model.
Do you have the item in a different color/size?
What you see on the product page is what's available. If you're unsure about sizing or options, email us before ordering and we'll help you pick the right one.
